====================================================================================================
DECISION RULE, stated before the numbers
====================================================================================================
  Protocol is contact_friction's, i.e. sim2real_proxy's: the frozen nominal forward
  model trained once on data/olie_train.npz, evaluated open-loop on each corner's own
  stream. Corners share SEEDS, i.e. the same initial condition -- not common random
  numbers: collect() draws sim.rng.random() only when the body has fallen, so streams
  desynchronise as soon as fall behaviour differs. Differences are unpaired, and the
  per-corner seed spread is published beside every verdict.
  Metrics per corner:
    within_0.2rad @100ms (roll/pitch only) -- the exact metric of the published negative;
    within_0.2rad per axis @100/500ms -- adds 500 ms and YAW, which it never scored;
    RMSE per axis, beside it -- a threshold crossing with unchanged error magnitude is
      not a moved axis;
    fall rate, regime mix, per-regime accuracy and an oracle at 500 ms -- the body/model split.
  Material = |mean delta| > max(3.0 pts, 2x the nominal seed spread OF THAT METRIC).
  Resolved = this corner's three seeds separate from nominal's three by more than that
    same bar. An axis is reported as moved only when it is material AND resolved; a wide
    corner spread is published beside every verdict, material or null.
  LIMITS: prediction accuracy, not policy transfer; and the twin's prediction across
  units, not a second real robot -- every real-log number here is one unit, one phone.
