DECISION RULE, stated before the numbers
====================================================================================================
  a CoM hypothesis is a body, so a candidate is a forward model trained on that body:
  35 candidates, dcom_x [-0.045, -0.03, -0.015, 0.0, 0.015, 0.03, 0.045] m x dcom_z [-0.02, -0.01, 0.0, 0.015, 0.03] m, each trained on
  100000 ticks collected with seed 1000 on the walk body, MLP(128) 30 epochs,
  torch seed 0. The grid extends one step beyond the published DR endpoints on both axes,
  so a truth at an endpoint is interior and a grid-edge argmin means the search ran out.
  score = servo_id.identify's normalised one-step error, on the first half of each hidden
  log (15000 ticks); band = 1.4826*MAD/2 of the errors on the two halves of
  the fit half; determined sets hold the other parameter at the argmin; a verdict is
  RESOLVED when every COUNTED hidden seed agrees and at least two are counted. Seeds are
  not paired (collect() draws sim.rng every tick for the push test, mode-dependently in
  Excitation, once per episode in fresh()).
  noise floor: a second nominal candidate (collection seed 1001, torch seed 0 like
  every candidate, so a lower bound on training noise) is scored on every hidden log with
  R = A; |err(nominal) - err(second nominal)| is the floor, the same number in A and B on
  the same stream. CONSUMED in both: a seed with floor / band >= 1, or band == 0, is
  below_noise and excluded from every identified / argmin / truth-in-set / diagonal tally
  and from the null case; the count excluded is published per body.
  null case: the nominal hidden body must identify to (0, 0) on every counted seed -- asserted.
  confound grid: dcom_x (7) x delay [0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6] x slew [2.0, None] x deadband [0, 2] deg
  = 196 hypotheses, scored on 3 hidden bodies x 3 seeds.
  limits: prediction accuracy, not policy transfer; every real-log number in this repo
  comes from ONE unit and ONE phone.
