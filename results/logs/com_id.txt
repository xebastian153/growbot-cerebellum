  a CoM hypothesis is a body, so a candidate is a forward model trained on that body:

  100000 ticks collected with seed 1000 on the walk body, MLP(128) 30 epochs,
  torch seed 0. The grid extends one step beyond the published DR endpoints on both axes,
  so a truth at an endpoint is interior and a grid-edge argmin means the search ran out.
  score = servo_id.identify's normalised one-step error, on the first half of each hidden
  log (15000 ticks); band = 1.4826*MAD/2 of the A/B quarter errors; determined
  sets hold the other parameter at the argmin; a verdict is RESOLVED when all three
  hidden seeds agree. Seeds are not paired (collect() draws sim.rng every tick for the
  push test, mode-dependently in Excitation, once per episode in fresh()).
  noise floor: a second nominal candidate (collection seed 1001) is scored on every
  hidden log; |err(nominal) - err(second nominal)| is the method's own training noise.
  null case: the nominal hidden body must identify to (0, 0) on every seed -- asserted.
  confound grid: dcom_x (7) x delay [0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6] x slew [2.0, None] x deadband [0, 2] deg
  = 196 hypotheses, scored on 3 hidden bodies x 3 seeds.
  limits: prediction accuracy, not policy transfer; every real-log number in this repo
  comes from ONE unit and ONE phone.
