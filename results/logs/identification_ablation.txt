== what the per-side split is and is not evidence for
  Read before quoting any per-side number:
  - the two per-side sweeps are ONE-DIMENSIONAL CONDITIONAL slices. Each is
    taken with the partner frozen at its coordinate-descent optimum and each
    is centred on its own argmin, cut with the band from the SHARED sweeps.
    Their disjointness therefore restates 'the two argmins differ by more than
    the band'; it is not independent evidence for an asymmetry.
  - the per-side fit is only separated from the shared fit when its fit gain
    exceeds that same band. Below the band, 'per-side fits better' is noise.

== what the sensor lag was costing the servo
  un-aligned (lumped) delay   5 ticks = 100 ms
  aligned (actuator + gyro)   4 ticks = 80 ms
  measured sensor lag         13.2 ms = 0.66 ticks
  the grid step is 20 ms, so a 13 ms correction can only move the
  argmin by a whole tick or not at all: it moved by 1.
  What is left after alignment is the actuator plus whatever absolute lag the
  gyro itself carries, which this log cannot measure. The decomposition is
  therefore a bound, not a split: the actuator is at most the aligned number.
