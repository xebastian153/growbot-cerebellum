== what the sensor lag was costing the servo
  un-aligned (lumped) delay   5 ticks = 100 ms
  aligned (actuator + gyro)   4 ticks = 80 ms
  measured sensor lag         13.2 ms = 0.66 ticks
  the grid step is 20 ms, so a 13 ms correction can only move the
  argmin by a whole tick or not at all: it moved by 1.
  What is left after alignment is the actuator plus whatever absolute lag the
  gyro itself carries, which this log cannot measure. The decomposition is
  therefore a bound, not a split: the actuator is at most the aligned number.
