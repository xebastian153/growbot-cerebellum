PART B -- decision rule, stated before the numbers
==============================================================================
  Protocol is sim2real_proxy's: the frozen nominal forward model, trained once on
  data/olie_train.npz, evaluated open-loop on each corner's own held-out stream.
  Two metrics per corner:
    within_0.2rad @100ms (roll/pitch only) -- the exact metric of the published
      negative, kept so the new corners and the old ones are comparable;
    within_0.2rad per axis @100/500ms -- adds YAW, which that metric never scored.
  Seeds are shared across every corner. Material = a shift from nominal larger
  than max(3.0 pts, 2x the nominal seed spread), the rule yaw_floor and real2sim use.

  training the frozen nominal model (60 epochs)...
  collected+scored: nominal (as shipped, condim 3)
  collected+scored: sliding 0.6 (published corner)
  collected+scored: sliding 1.4 (published corner)
